How We Work

From first call to launch

Four steps, defined deliverables, no surprises.

01
Discovery
A call to talk through your business, your goals, and who you're trying to reach. The more you share up front, the less back-and-forth later.
02
Design
A custom design built around your business, sent over for review before any code gets written. You give feedback. We revise until it's right.
03
Build
Clean, fast code built for mobile from day one. Speed and search fundamentals are built into the project, not tacked on at the end.
04
Launch
Testing on phones, tablets, and desktops. Once you approve it, the site goes live and everything is yours: the files, the logins, and a walkthrough of how it all works.
